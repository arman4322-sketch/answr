# Answr — Demand Validation Playbook

The single highest-ROI move before selling is **proof of demand**. A waitlist with real
signups reframes Answr from "an asset" to "an early-stage business" and can multiply the
sale price. This is the plan and the copy to get there fast and cheap.

The waitlist is live at **/waitlist** — every signup persists and appears in
**Settings › Leads**. Point all campaign traffic there.

---

## The 2-week validation sprint

**Goal:** 50–200 waitlist signups + a few reply-back conversations. That's enough signal.

| Days | Do |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Set `DEMO_PASSWORD` + a durable KV key so leads persist. Confirm /waitlist captures. |
| 1–5 | Cold outreach: 20–30 personalized emails/DMs per day to the ICP (below). |
| 3–7 | Post in communities where the ICP hangs out (below). Drive to /waitlist. |
| 5–10 | If budget allows: a small ($5–20/day) LinkedIn or Reddit ad to /waitlist. |
| 7–14 | Follow up with repliers; book 3–5 short calls. Note what they'd pay. |
| 14 | Tally signups + quotes. That number goes at the top of the sale listing. |

**What to measure:** signups, signup rate per channel, and any "I'd pay $X" quotes. Even
"here's a waitlist of 120 marketers + 3 who said they'd pay $99/mo" is a materially stronger
sale story than "pre-revenue asset."

## Who to target (ICP)

- **In-house SEO / content leads** at B2B SaaS and e-commerce brands (they own the "are we
  visible in AI answers?" question now).
- **Founders/heads of growth** at brands that live or die by discovery.
- **SEO/digital agencies** — they'd resell AEO to their whole book of clients (highest value).
- **AEO/GEO-curious marketers** already posting about ChatGPT/Perplexity visibility.

Where they gather: LinkedIn (search "AEO", "GEO", "AI search visibility"), r/SEO, r/bigseo,
indie-marketing Slacks/Discords, X/Twitter SEO circles, and newsletters' reply lists.

## Tips

- **Personalize the first line** of every cold email — reference their company/post. Generic
  = spam. 20 personalized beats 200 blasted.
- **Lead with the problem, not the product.** "Do you know what ChatGPT says about you?" is
  the hook.
- **Don't oversell** — you're validating, not closing. "Gauging interest" lowers the bar to reply.
- **Log every reply** and any price reaction — those quotes are gold for the listing.
- If a channel converts, spend a little to scale it before you list.
